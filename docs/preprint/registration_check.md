## Corrections that changed a registered item, and appendix B

- Session 8 (v0.4's plan): corrections 1 to 13 came before the registration was filed and are listed in it; none changed the four criteria, the calibration rule or the list of comparisons. Correction 5 changed how an input to H3 and H4 was obtained (the quantity read is the same). Appendix B names each group.
- After the registration: session 8's corrections 14 and 15 (descriptive checks of v0.4's result). They are deviations, not registered, and change no registered number. Appendix B states them; Section 10 reports the PubMedQA overlap check they added, labeled not registered.
- Session 9 (v0.5's plan): corrections 1 to 8 came before the update was filed and are part of what it registered. Appendix B lists each.
- After the update: session 9's correction 9 (a configuration file in the release folder) changed no registered item. Appendix B states it.
- Not written as corrections, and stated in appendix B: TabICLv2 NOT BUILT (K6's tabicl comparison not computed); vLLM's default build for CUDA 13.0 on session 9's GPU, as session 8's correction 1 provides for a driver of CUDA 13.0 or later, while session 9's plan had named the CUDA 12.9 build (the large model's code, revision, prompt and reading unchanged); s9_long_3 left without a calibration in session 9 and calibrated afterward (descriptive); and session 8's sharing of the large model's time by item count (the descriptive hand-off cost only).
- The embargo: the registration was filed under an embargo until 1 October 2027; the update said that the embargo would end on the day of v0.5's release. The release was public on 2 October 2026, and OSF showed the registration public when it was read at 01:06:40 UTC on 3 October 2026, the evening of 2 October in New York. Appendix B states it.
- Session 10's corrections 1 to 6 change no registered item. The usage check (corrections 5 and 6) is not a registered item; the preprint reports its second run (correction 6).

Every correction of sessions 8 and 9 that changed a registered item appears in appendix B.
